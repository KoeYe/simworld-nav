Three vision settings, compared

One world, three tellings. The tools are identical in all three; what changes is which facts the words are allowed to state outright.

none — direction, pedestrian lights and barriers exist only in the pictures.
route — the street the route takes is marked in the list; lights and barriers stay visual.
all — all three are in the text. A scripted text-only policy delivers 20 of 20 here, so this is the floor: a model that cannot do it is failing at the agent task, not at seeing.
